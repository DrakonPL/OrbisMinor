# Changelog

## 0.2.2

- **Switch buttons on the Switch.** The menus, the hints, and the CONTROLS page now show the Switch buttons (A, B, X, Y, L, R, +, −). Before, the Switch showed the PlayStation buttons.

## 0.2.1

### Gameplay

- **Gear up after takeoff.** The landing gear goes up when the ship is more than 30 m over the ground at 30 m/s or faster.
- **The ore field comes to you.** A planet has no ore field before your first dig there. The field then appears 25 to 40 m from that dig.
- **ORE and DIG ZONE marks.** The HUD mark ORE points to the nearest vein that still has ore. When all the veins are empty, the mark DIG ZONE shows your first dig.
- **Run with ore.** You can now walk and run at full speed with a chunk of ore in your hands.
- **The tool is held like a gun.** The suit holds the dig tool along the forearm, in the first-person and the third-person view.
- **A steady AUTO LAND prompt.** The prompt now stays on the screen when you fly low and slow over any ground. Over steep ground, the sea, or lava, a press shows NO FLAT GROUND for 2 seconds. Before, the prompt flickered over hills.
- **HEIST is not on the NEW GAME screen.** The mode is not ready yet. Each new game is an EXPEDITION.

### Fixes

- A dug hole no longer shows the sky for some frames while the ground around it loads.
- Traffic ships no longer land on your pad. An inbound ship that waits for 90 seconds jumps out.
- The hull of a capital ship now stops the shots of its own side.
- A bounty now pays only for a kill of its own target.
- An escort that is far from its leader keeps its place in the group.
- A field with a pirate base and a destroyer now has room for all of its pirate groups.
- A refused first dig on a planet no longer leaves a record in the save.
- A new best time now colors the TIME line.
- When a save fails, the menu shows SAVE GAME, not SAVE GAME IS OFF.
- PC: the arrow keys and the menu keys can no longer be bound to an action.
- The event banner is smaller when it has no hint line.
- Many smaller fixes to the HUD cards, the pirates, and the save.

## 0.2.0

### On foot (EVA)

- **Get out of the ship.** Land on a planet with the gear down, then hold DOCK to get out. Walk, run, and jump up to 200 m from the ship. Press DOCK near the hull, on any side, to board again.
- **The suit.** The explorer has a red and white suit with full animations: walk, run, jump, fall, land, carry, and aim.
- **First-person and third-person view.** VIEW ON FOOT changes the view. The game keeps your last choice.
- **The suit jet.** FIRE jumps. Hold FIRE in the air to fire the jet. The jet gets you out of a deep pit.
- **Wave.** RELEASE / WAVE waves the left hand.
- **Detailed ground.** On foot, the ground near you has much finer detail, and the trees and the rocks are solid.

### Dig and ore

- **The dig tool.** On foot, hold THRUST / DIG to dig and hold BRAKE / FILL to fill. You can dig pits and tunnels. The ship can land in a pit and fly through a tunnel.
- **Dig preview.** A ring shows where the tool will cut before you press. It is gold for a dig, cyan for a fill, and red when the dig is not possible. The suit tells you why.
- **Dig effects.** The tool has a beam, dust, rock chips, and a recoil.
- **DIG SIZE.** A new GAMEPLAY setting sets the tool radius to 2, 2.5, or 3 m.
- **Ore.** Each planet has one ore field with 2 to 4 veins. A dig into a vein drops chunks of ore. Press FIRE to pick up a chunk. Carry it to the ship to put it in the hold, which has 6 slots. Land on a starport pad or dock at a station to bank the hold.
- **The scan finds ore.** The wave of a scan pulse marks the ore field when it passes over it.
- **The digs are saved.** Your digs stay in the save, on up to 4 planets.

### Flight

- **Landing gear.** The ship now has three landing legs. They go down and up in 0.6 seconds. Use GEAR to move them.
- **Auto landing.** When you fly low and slow, the HUD shows AUTO LAND. Press DOCK, and the ship levels, slows down, and lands. DOCK, thrust, or a firm stick move cancels it.
- **FREE LOOK.** This is the new default stick layout. The right stick turns the camera round the ship, and the ship keeps its course. The camera goes back behind the ship after you release the stick.
- **Roll on the Vita.** With FREE LOOK, slide a finger sideways on the rear touch pad to roll. The new REAR ROLL setting gives DRAG, ZONES, or OFF.
- **Roll on the Switch and a PC pad.** With FREE LOOK, the shoulder buttons roll, and the triggers give thrust and brake.
- **SELF LEVEL.** A new CONTROLS setting levels the wings when you do not roll. AIR levels them in the air only. ALWAYS also levels them in space near a planet. OFF turns it off.

## Default controls for the new actions

| Action | Vita | Switch | PC keyboard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Get out (hold), board, auto land: DOCK | Triangle | X | F |
| Dig (hold): THRUST / DIG | R | ZR | Space |
| Fill (hold): BRAKE / FILL | L | ZL | Left Ctrl |
| Jump, pick up ore (hold in the air: suit jet): FIRE | Cross | B | J |
| Run: BOOST | Square | Y | Left Shift |
| Landing gear: GEAR | Circle | A | G |
| VIEW ON FOOT | D-pad right | D-pad right | V |
| RELEASE / WAVE | D-pad left | D-pad left | X |

You can change the buttons on the CONTROLS page.
